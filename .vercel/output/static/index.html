About Us

Rooted in Heritage,
Perfected by Hand

Nestled in Kanyakumari for over 25 years, Sree Meenakshi Handicrafts is a family-run workshop where every sculpture tells a story. From hand-carved teak wood to intricately polished soapstone jali work, our artisans pour their heart and soul into every contour.

We take pride in preserving traditional Indian craft. Each sculpture is 100% handmade, using time-honored techniques passed down through generations.
